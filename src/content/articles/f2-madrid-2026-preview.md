Formula 2 visits the Madring for the first time this weekend, **11-13 September 2026**, on a street circuit nobody in the field has raced. It is round eleven of fourteen.

That last detail matters, because the Formula 3 championship ends here and the two are easily confused. Formula 2 does not. After Madrid come Baku on 25-27 September, Lusail on 27-29 November and the finale at Yas Marina on 4-6 December.

## The standings

| # | Driver | Team | Points |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Nikola Tsolov | Campos Racing | 177 |
| 2 | Rafael Câmara | Invicta Racing | 166 |
| 3 | Gabriele Minì | — | 147 |
| 4 | Alexander Dunne | — | 118 |
| 5 | Noel León | — | 114 |

Eleven points. That is the margin the Bulgarian carries into a circuit with no history, after a Monza weekend where he finished fifth and sixth while Câmara's team locked out the top two places in the feature race.

## The weekend

| Session | Day | Local time |
|---|---|---|
| Practice | Friday 11 Sept | 10:00 |
| Qualifying | Friday 11 Sept | 12:55 |
| Sprint Race | Saturday 12 Sept | 13:10 |
| Feature Race | Sunday 13 Sept | 10:20 |

Qualifying on Friday lunchtime, on a track the drivers will have seen for exactly one hour beforehand. On street circuits that combination rewards the drivers willing to commit early and punishes everyone who needs a session to build up to it.

## What Tsolov needs

Not a win, necessarily. What he needs is to stop the bleeding.

He still has more victories than anyone else in the championship this season — the run that built his lead included the feature race at the Red Bull Ring and a last-lap pass at Silverstone. But four of the last six race results have gone Câmara's way in net terms, and eleven points is one bad Sunday.

With three rounds left after this one there is room to recover from a poor weekend. There is not room for two.

## Câmara's position

The Brazilian arrives with momentum and, on Monza's evidence, the quicker car. Invicta Racing lead nobody in the teams' standings — Campos are on 291 to Invicta's 244 — but the feature-race one-two suggested that gap reflects the first half of the season more than the current one.

Câmara is also the reigning Formula 3 champion, which means he has won a title at this level of the ladder before and knows what the closing rounds ask of a driver. Tsolov is chasing his first.

## The unknown

Nobody has data for the Madring. Set-ups come out of simulators, tyre behaviour is a guess until Friday afternoon, and a street circuit adds walls to every mistake.

For a championship leader defending eleven points, that is the least welcome kind of weekend: high variance, low information, and a rival who does not need anything dramatic to take the lead.
