The 2026 Formula 3 championship ends this weekend, **11-13 September**, at the brand-new Madring street circuit in Madrid. Freddie Slater leads by fifteen points. The format means that lead is worth considerably less than it sounds.

## The standings going in

| # | Driver | Team | Points |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Freddie Slater | Trident | 141 |
| 2 | Ugo Ugochukwu | Campos Racing | 126 |
| 3 | Ernesto Rivera | Campos Racing | 76 |

Rivera is 65 back with the season ending on Sunday. Barring something extraordinary, this is a two-man championship.

## Three races, not two

The finale does not follow the usual pattern. Formula 3 runs **a sprint race and two feature races** in Madrid, which puts far more points on the table than a normal round.

| Session | Day | Local time |
|---|---|---|
| Practice | Friday 11 Sept | 08:50 |
| Qualifying (two sessions) | Friday 11 Sept | 17:20 |
| Sprint Race | Saturday 12 Sept | 10:00 |
| Feature Race 1 | Saturday 12 Sept | 16:55 |
| Feature Race 2 | Sunday 13 Sept | 08:40 |

Qualifying is unusual too. Instead of one session, the field runs two back-to-back twenty-minute sessions on Friday evening: the first sets the grid for Saturday's sprint and first feature race, the second sets the grid for Sunday's closing race.

## Why fifteen points is not a cushion

Two feature races are worth 25 points each to a winner, with the sprint adding another ten, before pole positions and fastest laps. A driver having the weekend of his life can take more than sixty points out of Madrid.

Fifteen is less than a single feature-race victory. Slater cannot control this by finishing second everywhere; if Ugochukwu wins both features, second place three times is not enough.

Equally, the format cuts both ways. Ugochukwu needs three strong results, not one, and two of the three races start from grids set on Friday evening on a circuit nobody has driven.

## Trident's run

Slater drives for Trident, who have won the last three Formula 3 titles: Gabriel Bortoleto in 2023, Leonardo Fornaroli in 2024 and Rafael Câmara in 2025. A fourth in a row would make this the most dominant stretch any team has had in the championship's short history.

Câmara, the man who won it for them last year, is a paddock away this weekend, defending second place in Formula 2 against Nikola Tsolov. The ladder is short, and it shows.

## What decides it

Friday evening. Two qualifying sessions, both on an unknown street circuit, setting the grids for all three races. Whoever gets those twenty-minute runs right will spend the weekend in front — and on a track lined with walls, the driver in front is the one not stuck in traffic when someone else finds a barrier.
