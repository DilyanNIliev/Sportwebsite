Formula 1 arrives in Madrid this weekend for the first time. The Spanish Grand Prix runs from **Friday 11 to Sunday 13 September 2026** on the new MADRING street circuit at the IFEMA complex — a track that takes the Spanish Grand Prix away from Barcelona and drops it, as it happens, a short drive from Real Madrid's training ground.

It is round 14 of 23. Azerbaijan follows on 24-26 September.

## The weekend

| Session | Day | Local time |
|---|---|---|
| Free Practice 1 | Friday 11 Sept | 13:30 |
| Free Practice 2 | Friday 11 Sept | 17:00 |
| Free Practice 3 | Saturday 12 Sept | 12:30 |
| Qualifying | Saturday 12 Sept | 16:00 |
| Grand Prix | Sunday 13 Sept | — |

A debut circuit means nobody has real data. Set-ups come from simulators, tyre behaviour is a guess until Friday afternoon, and the drivers who adapt quickest on Friday tend to be the ones still there on Sunday.

## Formula 1: who can win

| # | Driver | Team | Points |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Kimi Antonelli | Mercedes | 267 |
| 2 | George Russell | Mercedes | 201 |
| 3 | Lewis Hamilton | Ferrari | 191 |
| 4 | Lando Norris | McLaren | 171 |

Antonelli comes to Madrid off the back of winning at Monza from nineteenth on the grid, and with a 66-point lead. On current form he is the favourite for the race as well as the championship.

Russell is the only realistic challenger for the title and drives the same car, which means he has to beat his team-mate on merit. Hamilton and Ferrari scored a single point at Monza and need the new circuit to suit them in a way the last few have not.

Max Verstappen took third at Monza and is the most likely winner from outside Mercedes. A street circuit with heavy braking zones is closer to Red Bull's strengths than Monza's straights were.

## Formula 2: Tsolov defends a narrow lead

| # | Driver | Team | Points |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Nikola Tsolov | Campos Racing | 177 |
| 2 | Rafael Câmara | Invicta Racing | 166 |
| 3 | Gabriele Minì | — | 147 |
| 4 | Alexander Dunne | — | 118 |
| 5 | Noel León | — | 114 |

Eleven points separate the Bulgarian from Câmara after a Monza weekend where Invicta locked out the front row of the feature race and Tsolov could only manage fifth and sixth.

Tsolov still has more wins than anyone in the field this season. What he no longer has is a comfortable margin, and Madrid is another circuit nobody has raced on.

## Formula 3: the title is decided here

| # | Driver | Team | Points |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Freddie Slater | Trident | 141 |
| 2 | Ugo Ugochukwu | Campos Racing | 126 |
| 3 | Ernesto Rivera | Campos Racing | 76 |

Madrid is the Formula 3 finale. Slater leads Ugochukwu by fifteen points with a sprint and a feature race left, which is not enough of a cushion to control — a bad Saturday and a strong Sunday from Ugochukwu would settle it the other way.

Rivera, 65 back, is out of it barring an extraordinary weekend.

Trident have won the last three Formula 3 titles — Bortoleto in 2023, Fornaroli in 2024, Câmara in 2025 — and Slater is racing to make it four. Câmara, meanwhile, is one paddock over, trying to take the Formula 2 title off Tsolov.

## What to watch

Three championships at different stages on one unknown circuit. One is effectively settled and looking for confirmation, one is down to eleven points, and one ends on Sunday.
