The 2026/27 Champions League league phase opened on Tuesday 8 September, and the tie everyone had circled delivered what the draw promised: Real Madrid 2-1 Inter at the Santiago Bernabéu, decided inside the first half.

## Fourteen minutes, then a gift

Kylian Mbappé scored the opener after fourteen minutes. It was the kind of goal that has stopped being surprising — and it carried a milestone with it. The France forward is now level with Raúl on 71 Champions League goals, fifth on the competition's all-time list.

The second was less about Madrid and more about Inter. Benjamin Pavard played a pass back towards his own goalkeeper. Martínez hesitated inside his six-yard box, and Fede Valverde, Madrid's captain, took the ball off him and turned it in.

Two goals, both inside the first half, both arriving from Inter mistakes rather than sustained Madrid pressure. Cristian Chivu's side had been the better team for stretches of that half and were two down at the end of it.

## Inter's response fell short

Carlos Augusto pulled one back after the interval with a finish struck hard enough to change the temperature of the match. For a period Inter looked like the side more likely to score again. They did not, and the Bernabéu held on to a scoreline that had been settled before half-time.

## Mourinho's first European night, again

The result carried a subplot that will follow this Madrid team all season. José Mourinho was appointed head coach in June 2026 on a three-year deal to 2029, replacing Álvaro Arbeloa — a return to the club he left in 2013, delivered on a promise Florentino Pérez made during his re-election campaign, and taken up after two seasons without a trophy.

His first Champions League night back at the Bernabéu ended in a win against a side that has reached two of the last four finals. It also ended with his team defending for the final half hour.
