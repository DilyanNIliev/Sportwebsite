This year's two biggest events have already happened. The Winter Olympics were held in Milano-Cortina from 6 to 22 February, and the World Cup ran through June and July. If you were waiting for the next one of that size, it is a long wait: the Olympics do not come back until Los Angeles in July 2028.

That does not mean the diary is empty. It means the shape of it has changed.

## What is left of 2026

Three things worth clearing an evening for. The Champions League returns on 13 October after a five-week gap and then runs almost fortnightly to Christmas. The ATP Finals take the eight best players of the tennis season indoors in Turin from 15 to 22 November. And Formula 1 ends on 6 December at Yas Marina, closing a twenty-four-round season that started in Australia in March.

## Why autumn 2027 is the one to circle

Next year has a pile-up that is worth seeing early.

The World Athletics Championships are in Beijing from 11 to 19 September. Two weeks later the Rugby World Cup starts in Australia — the first with twenty-four teams — and runs to 13 November. Three days after rugby begins, the Cricket World Cup starts in South Africa, Zimbabwe and Namibia and runs to 21 November.

Read those dates again. Rugby and cricket overlap almost completely: six weeks in which two different sports are each holding their biggest tournament at the same time, on opposite sides of the world, in opposite time zones. Anyone who follows both is going to spend October and November 2027 choosing.

Before all that, the Champions League knockout rounds run from mid-February to early May, and the final is on 5 June at the Metropolitano in Madrid.

## How to read the table

Below, the calendar has two views. **By month** shows the current month and the next one, worked out from the clock on whatever device you are reading this on — so it stays right without anyone updating it. **By year** lays out 2027 with the dates each event runs from and to.

One thing to be aware of. Only events whose dates we checked against two separate sources are in there, which makes the list shorter than a full fixture list and deliberately so. The WTA Finals, the Davis Cup, the closing MotoGP rounds and the darts world championship are all still to come this autumn and none of them is listed yet — one source each so far, and one calendar page we found had the Masters in November, which is four months out from where it actually is. Nothing from that page made it in.
