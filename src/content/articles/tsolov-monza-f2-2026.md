Nikola Tsolov came to Monza leading the Formula 2 championship and left still leading it. Between those two facts sat a weekend that did him no favours at all.

The tenth round of the season, held at the Autodromo Nazionale di Monza from 4 to 6 September in support of the Italian Grand Prix, belonged to Joshua Duerksen. The Invicta Racing driver won both races — a rare sweep — and the second of them by leading home a team-mate who happens to be Tsolov's closest title rival.

## The sprint: seventh to first

Duerksen started the sprint race seventh and finished it first. John Bennett took second and Oliver Goethe third. Tsolov brought his Campos Racing car home fifth, which is a points finish and, on a weekend where the pace was not there, a reasonable return.

The win gave Duerksen the record for the most Formula 2 podium finishes at Monza — a circuit that has been unusually kind to him.

## The feature race: an Invicta one-two

Sunday was worse for the championship leader. Duerksen went back-to-back, and Rafael Câmara followed him across the line to complete an Invicta Racing one-two. It was Duerksen's fourth win of 2026 and his eighth in Formula 2, equalling the series record.

Tsolov finished sixth.

Two races, eleven points scored, and a rival who scored eighteen in the feature race alone. The arithmetic was not kind.

Eleven points. That is a single race win, and rather less than the margin Tsolov carried into the weekend.

The Bulgarian still holds the most wins of anyone in the field this season — the run that took him to the top included victories at the Red Bull Ring and a last-lap pass at Silverstone that decided the sprint there. But momentum in a championship is measured in points, not in highlights, and at Monza the momentum changed hands without the lead doing the same.

Câmara is now close enough that a single bad Sunday for Tsolov would settle it. With the season heading into its closing rounds, the Bulgarian's task has narrowed from managing a lead to defending one.
